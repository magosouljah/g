# Recovery Knowledge

## Tracked baseline
- Sleep duration in hours.
- Sleep quality: 0–3.
- Energy: 0–3.
- Physical readiness: 0–3.
- Relevant soreness and discomfort/pain as notes rather than forcing every symptom into a numeric score.

## Scale
- 0 = very bad / clear problem
- 1 = bad
- 2 = normal / good
- 3 = very good

## Interpretation
Do not overreact to one poor day. Look for repeated changes in performance, readiness, pain and fatigue. Soreness can provide context but does not by itself determine whether a muscle is recovered.
