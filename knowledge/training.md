# Training Knowledge

Living knowledge base for the principles actually used by this system.

## Current operating principles
- Optimize hypertrophy stimulus relative to fatigue, not difficulty for its own sake.
- Track progression with repeatable technique, ROM, load, reps and RIR.
- Most compound work begins short of systematic failure; safe isolation work can be taken closer.
- Rest long enough to preserve productive performance.
- Volume and frequency are adjustable variables, not fixed weekly laws.
- Judge recovery primarily from performance trends, persistent fatigue/pain and repeated readiness patterns; soreness alone is not the main metric.
- Prioritize aesthetically important muscles while maintaining proportional development.

This file may evolve as evidence or our interpretation changes. Material changes that alter the actual program must also be recorded in `analysis/decisions.md`.
