# Health Knowledge

Health constraints outrank hypertrophy optimization when they conflict.

## Baseline context
- Male, age 22, height 1.90 m.
- Starting bodyweight reported around 60 kg.
- No known injuries reported.
- Usually around 8 hours sleep and generally wakes rested.

Given the low starting bodyweight for height, unexpected weight loss, inability to gain despite sustained adequate intake, significant symptoms, persistent pain or other concerning health changes should not be treated merely as programming problems and can justify professional medical evaluation.

Do not repeat generic warnings in routine training analysis when nothing relevant has changed.
