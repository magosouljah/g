# Hormones Knowledge

This section is for evidence relevant to natural hypertrophy and endocrine health, not for optimizing transient hormone spikes from workouts.

## Operating rules
- No TRT, anabolic steroids or anabolic drugs are part of the program.
- Do not program leg training under the assumption that acute post-exercise testosterone/GH spikes meaningfully drive torso hypertrophy.
- Distinguish chronic endocrine/health factors from short-lived exercise-induced hormonal changes.
- Health or hormone claims that could materially affect medical decisions require appropriately strong evidence and, when warranted, professional medical evaluation.
