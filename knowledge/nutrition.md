# Nutrition Knowledge

## Current baseline
- Current reported intake: approximately 3500 kcal/day and 150 g protein/day.
- Do not assume 3500 kcal is the correct long-term surplus.
- Adjust calories primarily from multi-day bodyweight trend plus training performance/recovery, not a single weigh-in or TDEE estimate.
- Do not increase protein merely for the sake of increasing it when current intake is already adequate for the goal.
- Favor sufficient carbohydrates for training performance, sufficient dietary fat, micronutrient adequacy and overall health.

Exact foods are not routinely logged initially. Add food-level or macro detail only when it can answer a useful question.
