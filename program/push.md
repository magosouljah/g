## How to record multiple sets
In `Load`, `Reps`, and `Actual RIR`, write sets in order separated by `/` (example: `60/60/60`, `10/9/8`, `2/1/1`).

## Warm-up
Warm-up sets are not work sets and are not logged unless relevant. Before the first heavy press, progressively ramp load without approaching failure.

## Execution
- Full, controlled ROM appropriate to the exercise.
- Do not deliberately shorten rest to create fatigue.
- No automatic partials/drop sets after failure.
- Stop according to target RIR unless safety or technique requires earlier termination.
